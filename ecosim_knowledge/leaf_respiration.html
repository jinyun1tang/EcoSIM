Leaf CO₂ and respiration in EcoSIM

Code-based knowledge note · 11 September 2026 · Sources refer to this repository checkout.

EcoSIM’s C3 CO₂ compensation point accounts for photorespiratory carbon loss. Maintenance and growth respiration are calculated separately. A conceptual leaf respiration term, Rd, can include respiration supporting both maintenance and growth; it should not be interpreted as maintenance respiration alone.

Three processes to distinguish

ProcessMeaningRepresentation in EcoSIM
PhotorespirationCarbon loss associated with Rubisco oxygenation.Included through the compensation-point correction in C3 fixation equations.
Maintenance respirationRespiratory carbon expenditure supporting existing tissue.Calculated separately using temperature and plant structural nitrogen.
Growth respirationRespiratory carbon expenditure associated with producing new biomass.Calculated separately with carbon and nutrient constraints and growth yields.

See PlantBranchMod.F90, approximately lines 3129–3213, for shoot maintenance and growth respiration calculations. These are branch-level calculations, not a direct identification of a leaf-only Rd variable.

What the compensation point includes

The C3 variable CO2CompenPoint_node is calculated as:

Γ* = ½ O₂ Vo,max Kc / (Vc,max Ko)

Here O₂ is dissolved oxygen concentration; Vo,max and Vc,max describe Rubisco oxygenation and carboxylation capacities; Kc and Ko are their kinetic constants. The code applies separate temperature responses to the two capacities. No mitochondrial respiration term appears in this calculation.

Thus Γ* represents the CO₂ concentration at which carboxylation balances photorespiratory CO₂ release, excluding other respiration. The asterisk makes that distinction explicit; the EcoSIM variable name itself does not include it. Source: StomatesMod.F90, lines 433–440.

How Rd relates to growth respiration

A conceptual leaf carbon-exchange equation is:

Anet = Vc − ½ Vo − Rd

In this notation, Rd denotes non-photorespiratory leaf respiration. It can include respiration supporting both maintenance and growth. It is not synonymous with growth respiration alone, nor with maintenance respiration alone.

At dissolved CO₂ concentration C = Γ*, the carboxylation and photorespiratory terms cancel. If Rd is positive, total net leaf uptake is still negative: Anet = −Rd. Consequently, the compensation point for total net exchange differs from Γ*.

Implementation qualification: Rd above is explanatory notation, not an explicit variable or subtraction in the inspected EcoSIM photosynthesis solver. EcoSIM handles maintenance and growth respiration in separate plant carbon calculations. Adding another Rd subtraction without tracing those budgets could count respiration twice.

Connection to internal CO₂ and photosynthesis

EcoSIM sets a reference internal CO₂ value using the PFT trait:

Ci,ref = (Ci/Ca)PFT Ca

This is LeafIntracellularCO2_pft, derived from CanopyCi2CaRatio_pft (the FCO2 trait). During C3 photosynthesis, a local value CO2X is adjusted until biochemical fixation approximately matches stomatal CO₂ supply:

Abiochemical(Ci) ≈ gCO₂(Ca − Ci)

The solver converts gaseous internal CO₂ into dissolved CO₂ using solubility, C = S(T)Ci, and calculates:

Ac = Vc,max max(0, C − Γ*) / (C + Km)
Aj = J max(0, C − Γ*) / (ELEC3 C + 10.5 Γ*)

The smaller rate determines fixation after water-stress, nutrient-feedback, and scaling factors. This fixation already includes the photorespiratory correction, but the local calculation does not explicitly subtract Rd. Higher internal CO₂ increases fixation with a saturating response when other conditions are fixed; stronger fixation can also draw internal CO₂ down when stomatal supply is fixed.

Sources: StomatesMod.F90, line 260; PhotoSynsMod.F90, lines 130–185. C4 mesophyll fixation uses a related balance with PEP carboxylation and a separate bundle-sheath calculation; the Γ* formula discussed above is the Rubisco/C3 compensation point.

Output interpretation

Ci_mesophyll_pft is populated from the reference LeafIntracellularCO2_pft, rather than the locally converged CO2X. It therefore does not directly report the water-stressed internal CO₂ used by the local photosynthesis calculation. Source: HistDataType.F90, approximately line 4854.
